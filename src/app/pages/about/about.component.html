About

Meet the teachers, the method, and the studio behind A-WELL Yoga in Sanford.

The Studio

A grounded yoga space in Sanford with room for both practice and healing.

A-WELL Yoga was built to feel personal, supportive, and honest. The work here includes weekly yoga classes, private sessions, workshops, retreats, and therapeutic offerings that help people feel more connected to their body instead of more disconnected from it.

Rather than treating yoga as performance, the studio is rooted in steadiness, awareness, breath, and a clear understanding that people practice for very real reasons: stress, recovery, clarity, resilience, and the need to feel better in their life.

That is what shapes the atmosphere here. Warm guidance. Thoughtful pacing. And practice that can meet someone whether they are brand new, returning, or looking for something deeper than a standard class.

The Approach

Traditional yoga, taught in a way that feels relevant to the nervous system and real life.

The foundation is still yoga: movement, breath, awareness, regulation, and presence. But the way it is taught is shaped by what helps people actually stay with themselves, rather than override themselves.

You will hear language around pacing, support, breath, embodiment, and the nervous system because the practice is meant to help people understand what is happening in their body, not just move through poses.

The result is a studio where gentle classes, therapeutic work, and deeper training can all live under one roof without losing warmth or clarity.

What People Come For

More than one reason to practice.

  • Weekly yoga classes in Sanford that feel approachable and supportive
  • Private sessions for more therapeutic, personalized guidance
  • Workshops and retreats for deeper immersion and reflection
  • Teacher training and continuing education shaped by Arieta’s method

What Holds It Together

A shared thread of steadiness, honesty, and care.

Whether someone arrives for a gentle class, a restorative practice, a NeuroYoga-based program, or a retreat, the intention is the same: help them feel more supported in their body and more capable of meeting life with clarity.
