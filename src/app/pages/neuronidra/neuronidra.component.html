Talk therapy engages the thinking brain — but what we carry is stored below the reach of language.

Grief. Anxiety. Overthinking. Old wounds. You may understand your patterns, know where they came from, even know what to do differently — and still feel your body react the same way. NeuroNidra™ works where words can't reach.

What is NeuroNidra™?

NeuroNidra™ is a guided practice that takes you from the busy, thinking mind into deep rest, where real change can begin. It works below words — somatic · vagal · neural: the body feels, the breath calms, the brain settles.

As you rest, your brain moves from beta (active thinking) to alpha (calm observing, where space opens) and toward theta (deep, dreamlike rest, where processing can unfold).

You never need to tell your story. I guide you. Your body does the work.

How it works

  1. Arrive — a gentle check-in: how you feel today, and what you need.
  2. Safety first — breath and grounding prepare the body to soften.
  3. Name it — body mapping and drawing help you find where emotions live, and name them.
  4. Gentle bilateral tapping — the butterfly hug, tapping or eye movements keep you anchored and present.
  5. Small, safe steps — gently turning toward what feels hard, always returning to safety.
  6. Process — guided deep rest where the body can release what it holds.

Who it's for

Anyone who feels stuck in stress, anxiety, grief, overthinking or old emotional patterns, and is ready to experience something beyond talking about it. No yoga experience needed — you'll be lying down, comfortable and supported, the whole time.

A practice, not a one-time fix

Healing isn't a one-time event. NeuroNidra™ is a practice you return to — each session builds on the last, helping your nervous system learn, again and again, that it is safe to rest.

About Arieta

Arieta Berisha Kirk is the creator of NeuroNidra™, a yoga therapist and anxiety coach (E-RYT 500) with an academic background in psychology and neuroscience. For more than 20 years she has guided yoga, breathwork and mindfulness across many communities, with over 15,000 teaching hours. She is the founder of the RESET Method™ and A-Well Yoga School.

NeuroNidra™ is a wellbeing practice and a complement to — not a replacement for — psychological or medical care.
